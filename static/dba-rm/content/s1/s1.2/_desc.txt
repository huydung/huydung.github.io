# Types of Research

Research takes many forms, each suited to different questions and contexts. Understanding the landscape helps you choose appropriate methods for your inquiry and evaluate whether a study's approach matches its aims. Some research describes what exists; other research explains why. Some builds theory; other solves immediate problems. Some relies on numbers; other captures narrative and experience.

The types aren't mutually exclusive—comprehensive investigations often combine multiple types. An experimental intervention study (testing a practical solution) might pair with a quantitative correlation study (examining causal mechanisms) and a qualitative exploration (documenting experience). Together, these approaches provide a multi-dimensional understanding that neither quantitative nor qualitative methods alone could achieve.
