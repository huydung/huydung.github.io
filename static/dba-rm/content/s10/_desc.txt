## Writing & Feedback: Communicating Findings With Clarity and Impact

Research findings matter only if they're communicated clearly and compellingly to audiences who can understand and act on them. Excellent research coupled with poor writing wastes intellectual investment; conversely, clear writing elevates good research into impactful contributions. Writing is both a tool for communicating results and a tool for thinking through findings—the act of writing often reveals gaps, contradictions, or insights that weren't visible in raw data. Feedback loops between writer and readers (peers, mentors, instructors) strengthen writing through iterative refinement. Clear writing on both quantitative findings (methods, tables, effect sizes) and qualitative findings (narratives, themes, representative quotes) ensures that stakeholders understand not just what was found but why it matters. This section covers foundational writing skills, methods for presenting findings with rigor and clarity, and strategies for receiving and integrating feedback.
