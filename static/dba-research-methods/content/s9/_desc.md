## Ethics: Protecting People and Maintaining Research Integrity

Research ethics is not an afterthought or compliance box—it's foundational to credible, respected inquiry. Ethics protects human subjects from harm, preserves researcher integrity, and ensures findings serve stakeholders responsibly. Ethical research explicitly addresses conflicts of interest, avoids bias favoring particular outcomes, and transparently handles data about real people. Good ethical practice manifests across all research types: random assignment and control conditions are designed with informed consent and minimal harm; surveys maintain participant confidentiality while transparently reporting findings; interviews obtain IRB approval, build trust through informed consent and anonymity, and ensure findings are presented honestly without distortion.
