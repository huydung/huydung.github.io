## Validity & Reliability: Ensuring Your Research Findings Are Trustworthy

In research, **validity** answers the question: Are you measuring what you claim to measure? **Reliability** asks: Are your measurements consistent and stable? Together, they form the foundation of credible research. Validity ensures your study actually captures the construct of interest—whether internal validity (did the treatment cause the effect?), external validity (do results generalize?), or construct validity (are your measures accurate?). Reliability ensures measurements are consistent across time, raters, or items. In any research on organizational phenomena, both quantitative reliability (Cronbach's alpha values) and qualitative credibility mechanisms (member checking, chain of evidence) strengthen confidence in findings about how various factors shape team dynamics and outcomes.
