## Reliability: Ensuring Consistent, Reproducible Measurement

Reliability concerns whether your measurement tools and processes produce consistent results. A reliable thermometer gives the same reading when measuring the same temperature repeatedly; an unreliable one fluctuates wildly. In research, reliability has two core meanings: In quantitative work, it's about **consistency of measurement**—do multiple items on a survey consistently capture the same construct? Do raters independently arrive at the same score? In qualitative work, it's about **consistency of process**—are data collection and analysis procedures documented and reproducible so another researcher following the same protocol would reach similar conclusions? High reliability doesn't guarantee validity (you could consistently measure the wrong thing), but it's necessary: without reliable measures, validity becomes impossible.
