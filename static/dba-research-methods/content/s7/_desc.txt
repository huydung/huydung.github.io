# Data Analysis

Data analysis is the systematic process of inspecting, cleaning, transforming, and modeling data to extract meaningful insights and answer research questions. Whether from quantitative surveys, qualitative interviews, or longitudinal observations, data analysis bridges raw evidence into findings that address hypotheses and themes. The approach varies fundamentally by methodology: quantitative analysis relies on statistical hypothesis testing and effect size estimation, while qualitative analysis uses iterative coding cycles to identify patterns, themes, and meaning. Mixed-methods analysis integrates both approaches through joint displays and sequential logic, enabling researchers to explain mechanisms and test emerging insights against empirical evidence.

Effective data analysis requires clarity on research questions, appropriate tool selection, and rigorous verification of findings. Whether through SPSS for descriptive statistics, SmartPLS for structural equation modeling, or systematic thematic coding by hand or software, the goal remains the same: to reduce complexity, uncover relationships, and present evidence in forms that resonate with audiences seeking actionable insights.
